# Flux 1.4 — 2026-05-31

Four user-facing additions and a substantial accuracy fix. Flux can now put a price on your energy — per-day and per-period costs computed from time-of-use bands you manage in Settings. The app reflows properly on iPad and on macOS instead of stretching the iPhone layout. The Dashboard tells you when the battery can't drain before off-peak. And the peak/off-peak grid-import split — which previously misread by several kWh some days — is now computed directly from the 10-second readings and shown consistently everywhere, including today.

## What's New

### Daily Costs (T-1326)

Day Detail gains a costs card with four rows: **peak imports** (what grid energy cost you), **solar feed-in income**, **net**, and **off-peak savings**. The History range view gains a four-tile **Period Costs** card covering the selected range, with an "N of M days priced" caption when some days in the range fall outside your configured pricing.

Pricing is yours to define. A new **Settings → Pricing** screen lets you add, edit, and delete time-of-use bands, each with a peak import rate, a solar feed-in rate, and an off-peak savings rate (to 4 decimal places). The editor validates as you go and shows a targeted banner for each problem — inverted dates, overlapping periods, rates out of range, a second open-ended period — including a one-tap fix that closes the current open-ended period on the date you just picked.

Costs are computed in the app from the existing `/day` and `/history` responses, so no chart or data shape changes. The pricing periods themselves live in a new `flux-pricing` table, with `GET`/`POST`/`PUT`/`DELETE /pricing` (plus a `replace-open-ended` endpoint) behind the existing bearer-token auth. The "at most one open-ended period" rule is enforced race-safely across devices.

### iPad adaptive layout (T-1150)

On iPad at regular width — full-screen, and half-or-more of a Split View — Flux now uses a proper **sidebar** (Dashboard / Today / History) with a detail column, instead of stretching the iPhone tab-bar layout edge to edge. Each screen reflows into multiple columns as the detail width allows: the Dashboard places the battery hero beside the live readout and grids the summary blocks; History grids its solar / grid / daily-usage / selected-day cards under a full-width overview; Day Detail splits into a summary column and a stacked-chart column.

Settings opens as a sheet from a toolbar gear (capped to a readable width so form rows aren't stretched). At compact widths — Slide Over and narrow Split View — iPad falls back to the existing tab-bar shell, and iPhone Plus/Max landscape stays on the iPhone layout. iPhone is unchanged.

### macOS interface refresh (T-1342)

The macOS app now uses the same adaptive multi-column layout as iPad rather than the in-content iPhone chrome. Day Detail's date moves into the **window title**, and previous/next-day navigation moves into the **toolbar** (← / → still work from the keyboard). Windows open at a comfortable default size and won't shrink below a sensible minimum.

### Battery "won't empty before off-peak" indicator (T-1327)

The Dashboard hero now answers a question the "empty by HH:MM" estimate couldn't: *is there any way the battery drains before off-peak starts?* When it can't reach the cutoff before the next off-peak window opens — even assuming a maximum sustained discharge — the hero shows **"Won't empty before HH:MM"**, led by the live charge/discharge rate (e.g. `Discharging · 400 W · won't empty before 11:00`). It's an informational state, deliberately not styled as a cutoff warning. When the battery is idle or live data is missing, it falls back to the plain text.

## Fixes

### Accurate peak and off-peak grid import (T-1341, T-1420, T-1421)

The off-peak split and the daily peak grid-import figure are now computed by **integrating the 10-second readings series** over the relevant windows, replacing the previous approach of subtracting two AlphaESS energy snapshots taken at the window boundaries.

AlphaESS's intraday energy counter lags physical reality by 15–30 minutes, so the 14:00 boundary snapshot routinely missed the tail of grid-charging and that energy was misclassified as peak. The effect could be large: on 2026-05-18 the OVO meter reported **0.17 / 20.75 kWh** peak/off-peak for the day, while Flux reported **1.74 / 18.95** — roughly a 4× overcount on the peak imports tile, even though the daily total matched to about 1 %.

With this release:

- **Off-peak deltas** (grid usage, grid export, solar, battery charge/discharge) are integrated from readings, and window-end finalisation waits for a reading at or after the off-peak end before committing, so a missing boundary reading defers rather than recording a partial window.
- **Peak grid import** is a single **server-computed value** integrated directly from grid-import power over the two windows bracketing off-peak. It's shown consistently on the **Dashboard, Day Detail, and History** — and for **today** it updates live through the day, rather than appearing only after the off-peak window opens.
- **Historical days** within the 30-day readings window are corrected by a backfill CLI.

### "Won't empty before" hero line now shows the power-flow rate

The off-peak indicator above previously dropped the live charge/discharge rate that the normal status line carries; it now leads with the same `Discharging · X` / `Charging · X` prefix so the two lines can't disagree.

## Upgrade Notes

No breaking changes. The new `flux-pricing` DynamoDB table and the `/pricing` endpoints are additive; existing endpoints and data are unchanged. To see costs, configure at least one pricing period in Settings → Pricing. Historical peak grid-import figures backfill within the 30-day readings retention window; days older than that keep the previous residual estimate.
